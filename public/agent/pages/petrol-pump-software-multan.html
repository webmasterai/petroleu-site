Petrol pump software in Multan

Multan sits at the heart of southern Punjab transport corridors, where fuel demand shifts with harvest seasons, freight movement, and urban growth. Petroleu supports Multan petrol pumps with integrated nozzle readings, tank dipping, credit management, and shift closing built for Pakistani operations. Attendants enter meter readings; the system produces nozzle-wise sales and variance signals before daily closing is approved. Dip readings on underground tanks help owners compare physical stock with book figures after busy sales days. Credit customers — from local transporters to agri businesses — get tracked balances and vehicle-wise billing. Cloud and offline modes suit stations with varying connectivity, while mobile dashboards keep owners informed across Multan and nearby towns.
