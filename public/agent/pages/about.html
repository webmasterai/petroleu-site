About Petroleu

Petroleu is petrol pump and fuel station management software developed by Digital Softs for operators across Pakistan.

We help station owners digitize nozzle readings, tank dipping, credit billing, shift closing, and financial reporting so daily forecourt work stays organized and auditable.

Digital Softs has supported fuel retail operators for years with software that matches real Pakistani pump workflows rather than generic accounting alone.

Our mission

Give every petrol pump owner clear visibility into sales, stock, cash, credit, and accounts — from the forecourt to the owner’s mobile phone.
