Why fuel stations in Pakistan choose Petroleu

Petroleu is petrol pump management software built for Pakistani fuel stations. Owners use it to manage nozzle readings, tank dipping, stock control, credit customers, shift closing, accounts, payroll, and daily reports from one cloud platform. Whether you operate a single forecourt in Lahore or a multi-branch network, Petroleu replaces scattered registers with searchable records and mobile visibility for owners who cannot stay on-site all day.

Main features

  • Nozzle Reading Management
  • Tank Dipping & Stock Control
  • Cash & Credit Sales
  • Customer Credit Management
  • Daily Closing Reports
  • Accounts & Ledgers
