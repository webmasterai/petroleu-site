Frequently asked questions

Frequently asked questions about Petroleu petrol pump management software — pricing, nozzle readings, tank dipping, credit, mobile access, and reports.

General

What is Petroleu petrol pump software? — Petroleu is petrol pump management software by DigitalManager. It helps fuel station owners manage nozzle readings, tank stock, credit customers, cash and credit sales, daily closing, accounts, reports, and mobile monitoring from one system.

How can Petroleu help my petrol pump business? — Petroleu keeps daily pump operations organized. Owners and managers can record nozzle readings, track fuel stock, manage customer ledgers, review cash and credit sales, and check daily closing reports without depending only on paper registers.

Nozzle Reading

What is nozzle reading in petrol pump software? — Nozzle reading means recording opening and closing meter values on each dispenser. Petrol pump software uses these readings to work out litres sold per nozzle, per shift, and per day — the basis for accurate sales and variance tracking.

How does Petroleu calculate nozzle sales? — Petroleu subtracts opening meter readings from closing readings for each nozzle. The difference gives litres sold, which is multiplied by the fuel rate for sale value. Results feed into shift and daily closing reports without manual calculation.

Tank Dipping & Stock

What is tank dipping in petrol pump software? — Tank dipping measures actual fuel level in underground tanks, usually with a dip stick and calibration chart. Petrol pump software compares dip readings with system stock to show gain, loss, or leakage that meter sales alone might miss.

How does Petroleu manage fuel tank stock? — Petroleu tracks opening stock, purchases, nozzle sales, and closing stock per tank. Dip entries update physical levels, and the system highlights variance between book stock and dipped stock for each product.

Credit Customers

Can Petroleu manage credit customers? — Petroleu maintains a credit customer directory with limits, balances, and payment history. Staff record credit sales at the nozzle or POS, and outstanding amounts update on customer ledgers as entries are saved.

Can Petroleu manage vehicle-wise credit billing? — Vehicles link to credit accounts in Petroleu, so each fill records vehicle number, litres, and amount. Fleet owners receive clear vehicle-wise billing instead of one lump sum that is hard to reconcile.
