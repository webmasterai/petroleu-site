# Petrol pump software in Mardan

Mardan's growing fuel retail market serves urban commuters, regional transport, and credit customers who need dependable billing discipline. Petroleu supports Mardan petrol pumps with integrated nozzle reading software, tank dipping, daily closing, and customer credit management. Attendants record readings; supervisors review variance before approving shift close. Stock control links nozzle sales to tank levels with dip reconciliation after physical measurements. Accounts for cash, bank, suppliers, and expenses connect to daily operations — reducing gaps between forecourt activity and bookkeeping. Owners use Petroleu mobile access to review sales and outstanding credit from anywhere in Mardan.
