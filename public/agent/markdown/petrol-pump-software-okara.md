# Petrol pump software in Okara

Okara pumps serve canal-belt agricultural traffic, route travellers, and local credit buyers who expect transparent fuel billing. Petroleu brings petrol pump management software to Okara with workflows for nozzle readings, dipping, credit customers, accounts, and mobile owner dashboards. Nozzle sales link to tank stock automatically, reducing manual reconciliation at day end. Dip gain and loss tracking helps investigate shortage when book and physical stock diverge. Credit customer software supports vehicle-wise entries and monthly statements for fleet accounts. Okara owners use Petroleu to modernize operations while keeping the familiar rhythm of shift-based forecourt work.
