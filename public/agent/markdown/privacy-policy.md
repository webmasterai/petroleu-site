## Information we collect

We collect account and identity information (name, email, company, role), business records you enter (sales, tank dipping, credit, payroll, expenses), and technical logs needed to operate the service securely.

Petroleu is B2B software for petrol pump operators and authorized staff; we do not target consumer personal use.

Data is processed to provide the Service, support customers, improve reliability, prevent fraud, and meet legal obligations. We do not sell customer business data.

We use encryption in transit, access controls, and backups. Records are retained while your subscription is active and as required by law.

You may request access, correction, or deletion of personal data by contacting support@petroleu.com. We respond within reasonable timeframes under applicable law.
