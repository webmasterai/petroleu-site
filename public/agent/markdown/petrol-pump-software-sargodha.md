# Petrol pump software in Sargodha

Sargodha sits in central Punjab where fuel retail supports farming logistics, regional transport, and growing town traffic. Petroleu brings petrol pump management software to Sargodha stations — combining nozzle readings, dipping, stock, credit customers, and accounts on one platform. Staff record shift entries once; daily closing compiles cash, credit, POS, and expenses without manual totals. Tank dipping software tracks gain and loss after each physical reading. Credit customer software separates cash sales from receivables so collection teams know exactly what is outstanding. Owners review reports on desktop or mobile, keeping control even when attending to other business in Sargodha.
