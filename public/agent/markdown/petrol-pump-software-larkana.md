# Petrol pump software in Larkana

Larkana fuel stations play a vital role for interior Sindh transport, local retail, and established credit customer relationships. Petroleu helps Larkana owners digitize nozzle readings, tank dipping records, daily closing, and customer outstanding balances in one system. Shift staff enter readings; managers reconcile cash responsibility and sales splits before locking the day. Tank dipping highlights differences between dipped and system stock — a practical tool for shortage investigation. Credit customer reports show who owes what, supporting reminders and monthly billing. Petroleu mobile access lets owners monitor sales and stock without being physically present on every shift.
