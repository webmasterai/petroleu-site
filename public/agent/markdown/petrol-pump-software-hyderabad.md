# Petrol pump software in Hyderabad

Hyderabad pumps anchor fuel retail for southern Sindh — serving local traffic, regional hauliers, and credit buyers who expect accurate records. Petroleu provides fuel station management software with nozzle reading software, dipping, stock control, and daily closing built for Pakistani pumps. Meter readings per nozzle roll into shift and day reports automatically, reducing transcription errors common with paper registers. Tank stock for petrol and diesel stays updated with purchases, sales, and dip adjustments. Credit customer billing supports vehicle-wise entries and outstanding balance tracking. Hyderabad owners use cloud sync and mobile views to stay connected to station performance while managing staff accountability across shifts.
