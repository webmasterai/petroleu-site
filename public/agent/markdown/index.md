# Petroleu — Petrol Pump Software Pakistan | Fuel Station Management

Petroleu: cloud petrol pump management for Pakistan — real-time stock, nozzle sales, dipping, customer credit, payroll & financial reports. Demo & pricing.

## Product overview

Petroleu is **petrol pump software** and **fuel station management software in Pakistan**. Run your fuel station smarter with Petroleu. Manage nozzle readings, tank stock, credit customers, daily closing, accounts, reports, and mobile monitoring from one system.

## Main features

- Nozzle reading management
- Tank dipping and stock control
- Cash and credit sale tracking
- Customer ledgers and vehicle-wise billing
- Daily closing and shift reports
- Mobile owner dashboard

## Pakistan market

Petroleu supports petrol, diesel, HOBC, lubricants, credit customers, supplier purchases, shift closing, and financial reports for fuel stations across Pakistan.
