# Petrol pump software in Lahore

Lahore petrol pumps serve one of Pakistan's busiest urban markets, where multiple shifts, credit fleets, and fast fuel rate changes are part of daily life. Petroleu is petrol pump software in Lahore built around real forecourt workflows — nozzle meter readings, tank dipping, customer credit, and daily closing reports in one system. Attendants record opening and closing readings per nozzle; the software calculates sales and highlights variance before the day is locked. Tank stock for petrol, diesel, and HOBC stays visible with dip gain and loss tracking. Credit customers across transport firms and local businesses get proper ledgers and vehicle-wise billing. Lahore owners who manage more than one site rely on cloud sync and mobile dashboards to stay informed without standing on the forecourt all day.
