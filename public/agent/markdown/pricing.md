# Petroleu pricing

Transparent PKR pricing for petrol pump software in Pakistan. Choose Lite, Professional, Automation, or multi-station plans based on your forecourt size and modules required.

Lite starts from PKR 1999 per month. Professional and Automation plans add payroll, WhatsApp, AI reporting, and hardware integration where available.

## Lite Version

PKR 1999 per month. Perfect for single-location stations

- For 1 user account
- Purchase: fuel and lubricants
- Sale: nozzle sale, bulk sale, credit sale, card sale
- Inventory: tank stock, dip gain/loss, lube stock

## Professional Version

PKR 3999 per month. For professional accountants and finance managers who need better control over petrol station operations.

- Up to 5 user accounts
- Purchase: fuel and lubricants
- Sale: nozzle sale, bulk sale, credit sale, card sale
- Inventory: tank stock, dip gain/loss, lube stock

## Automation Station Version

PKR 6999 per month. 

- Up to 5 user accounts
- Live sale monitoring with dispenser nozzle integration
- Live tank stock monitoring with ATG
- Purchase: fuel and lubricants

## Multi Station Sites

PKR call for special prices per month. 

- Multi-station fuel network management
- Purchase: fuel and lubricants
- Sale: nozzle sale, bulk sale, credit sale, card sale
- Inventory: tank stock, dip gain/loss, lube stock
