# Petrol pump software in Jhang

Jhang petrol pumps support central Punjab transport, agricultural activity, and local credit buyers who rely on consistent billing records. Petroleu streamlines nozzle reading entry, tank dipping, stock tracking, and shift-wise daily closing for Jhang fuel stations. The software calculates sales from meter readings and connects them to tank stock movement automatically. Credit customer software manages limits, vehicle-wise fills, and outstanding balances with printable monthly bills. Dip gain and loss entries give owners a clear trail when physical stock does not match book figures. Mobile dashboards help Jhang owners stay informed while managing other responsibilities away from the pump.
